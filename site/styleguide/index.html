Sizes and space

02

Colour

Four colours and their transparencies, nothing else. Every surface, line and text tone is one of these.

Transparencies of white, on ink

Contrast: red type on ink is about 3.2:1, so red is used for large type and shapes only; small text is white or its transparencies.

Prototype notes. Calm and functional; with reduced motion turned on, every loop stops and the hero shows its last frame.
